### Table Policy
Tables must be manned at all times while the Artist Alley and Exhibitor Hall is open.  The individual who purchased the table must be present at the event.  Proxy selling is strictly prohibited.  Outside of emergencies, early breakdown is prohibited and will result in a permanent ban from future MiniCon events.  All individuals present behind a table must have a valid MiniCon vendor badge.  Additional badges may be purchased for a $10 fee per badge.  A maximum of 4 people can be behind a single, 6ft table, due to safety precautions.

Vendors must have tables set up by 11am when the hall opens.  Vendors who arrive past 11am will not be allowed to set up and will not be given any refunds.  Table displays must not obstruct the pathways or intrude in a neighboring table’s space.  Displays must be secured and be at a reasonably safe height.  Unsecured displays will be asked to be taken down if MiniCon staff deems it a safety risk.  Tables are prohibited from playing music or using excessively bright, LED lighting.  Flashing lights are prohibited. Staples, nails, tacks, and other tapes are strictly prohibited on tables, floors and walls.

### Sales Tax & Insurance
Vendors are liable for collecting and submitting their own sales taxes to the state of New Jersey.  MiniCon holds no responsibilities to collect sales taxes.


Individual vendor insurance is not required but is highly advised.  MiniCon is not responsible for any damages, theft or losses during the duration of this event, and vendors understand and agree that they will be vending at their own risk if they do not have individual insurance.

### Prohibited Items & Goods
MiniCon is committed to providing lawful and authentic goods through our vendors.  Therefore, we prohibit the sale or distribution of any counterfeit, bootleg, knock-off, stolen, pirated, or copied items.

As MiniCon is also a family-friendly event, the sale or distribution of explicit material is also prohibited. This includes any material that may display any sexually explicit or graphically violent images unsuitable for young children or minors. Material with heavy religious or political themes is also prohibited for the safety and comfort of our attendees.

Prohibited Items:
- Unlicensed, unofficial or bootleg items of official goods, including but not limited to: bluerays/DVDs/CDs/albums, posters, toys, plush, blind boxes, jewelry, or accessories.
- AI-generated images/stolen images/traced or copied images
- Fanart (exhibitor vendors)
- Fanart using officially licensed assets or logos (Artist Alley vendors)
- Weapons or props that do not comply with our weapons policy, or any other harmful items (exhibitors only)
- NSFW/18+ goods
- Food items and other consumables without proper food licenses (exhibitors only)
- Any illegal substances

Any vendor caught with any prohibited items will be asked to remove said items from their display. Non-compliance will result in being escorted off the premises with no refunds, and a permanent ban from future MiniCon events.

### Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy
MiniCon has a strict anti-AI policy for our Artist Alley and Exhibitors. MiniCon is dedicated to supporting authentic, human-made goods. As such, items or content that use AI-generated images are strictly prohibited.  Any vendor caught displaying or selling items featuring AI-generated images will be asked to remove said items from their display. Non-compliance will result in being escorted off the premises with no refunds, and a permanent ban from future MiniCon events.

### Sales of Food and Other Consumable Items
Exhibitor vendors with appropriate food certifications are permitted to sell pre-packaged food items at MiniCon.  Artist Alley vendors are not permitted to sell food items, and must apply for an exhibitor vendor table if they wish to sell food items with proper certification.

### Food Regulations for Exhibitor Vendors (not food trucks):
- Exhibitor vendors must submit an approved/valid NJ Temporary Retail Food License and/or NJ Cottage Food Operator Permit **at least 2 weeks prior to the event**
- Labeling: All food must display clear ingredient labels to inform participants of allergens and dietary restrictions
- All foods must be pre-packaged for individual sale
- Commercially prepackaged, shelf-stable food items (e.g., packaged cookies, candy bars) and beverages may be sold or distributed without a Food Handler’s Certification.  Goods labeled with “not for resale” are prohibited.
- Foods containing alcohol, CBD, THD or similar products are not permitted
- Foods that are susceptible to spoilage, require set temperature control, or have risk of growing bacteria or disease are not permitted for sale. These include, but are not limited to:
  - Meat and fish
  - Fresh dairy (milk, cream, cheese, custard, cream cheese, sour cream, pudding, buttercream, meringue, whipped cream, ganache, etc)
  - Vegetables (fresh, cooked, fried)
  - Fresh fruit
  - Fresh flowers
  - Cooked rice and beans
  - Tofu
- Example of accepted food items include:
  - Baked goods
  - Candy and fudge
  - Chocolate-covered nuts and dried fruit
  - Dried baking mixes, granola mixes, trail mixes, nut mixes
  - Jams/jellies/preserves
  - Fruit pies, fruit empanadas, fruit tamales (no meat, fish or vegetable products)
  - Roasted coffee or dried tea
  - Popcorn and caramel popcorn

### Food Regulations for Food Trucks:
- Must submit proof of Food Protection Manager Certification
- Must submit approved copy of the Mobile Food Unit (Peddler) License from the City of Newark Office of Central Licenses & Permits which includes:
  - Health Certificate
  - Fire Permit
  - Food Handler's Certificate
  - Physician's Certificate
  - Code Enforcement approval
- All paperwork must be submitted **at least 2 weeks prior to the event**
