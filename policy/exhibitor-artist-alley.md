## Exhibitor and Artist Alley Policies

### Prohibited Items & Goods
MiniCon is committed to providing lawful and authentic goods through our vendors.  Therefore, we prohibit the sale or distribution of any counterfeit, bootleg, knock-off, stolen, pirated, or copied items.

As MiniCon is also a family-friendly event, the sale or distribution of explicit material is also prohibited. This includes any material that may display any sexually explicit or graphically violent images unsuitable for young children or minors. Material with heavy religious or political themes is also prohibited for the safety and comfort of our attendees.

Prohibited Items:
- Unlicensed, unofficial or bootleg items of official goods, including but not limited to: bluerays/DVDs/CDs/albums, posters, toys, plush, blind boxes, jewelry, or accessories.
- AI-generated images/stolen images/traced or copied images
- Fanart (for exhibitor vendors)
- Weapons or props that do not comply with our weapons policy, or any other harmful items
- NSFW/18+ goods
- Food items and other consumables without proper food licenses
- Any illegal substances

Any vendor caught with any prohibited items will be asked to remove said items from their display. Non-compliance will result in being escorted off the premises with no refunds, and a permanent ban from future MiniCon events.

### Artificial Intelligence (AI) Policy
MiniCon has a strict anti-AI policy for our Artist Alley and Exhibitors. MiniCon is dedicated to supporting authentic, human-made goods. As such, items or content that use AI-generated images are strictly prohibited.  Any vendor caught displaying or selling items featuring AI-generated images will be asked to remove said items from their display. Non-compliance will result in being escorted off the premises with no refunds, and a permanent ban from future MiniCon events.
