## Want to be a Panelist?
MiniCon is a celebration of people’s passions and interests, and it strives to foster a welcoming and supportive community for all kinds of fans.  If you have a topic or workshop you would love to present at MiniCon 2027, please submit an application before the listed deadline.

**Panelist/Workshop applications are OPEN.  Please apply [here](https://forms.gle/TnRFcWgkcYfgjKSm8)!**

Panel/Workshop application deadline: January 20th, 2027

### Panel Policies
- Panelists are unpaid, volunteer positions.
- Panelists must be at least 18 years of age.
- Panelists will receive a complimentary badge, up to two (2) badges.
- Panel and workshop applications **are not** first-come, first-served.
- Consumable food items (ie: candy) cannot be distributed during your panel due to health risks.
- Panels with sensitive or controversial topics must be labelled with clear content warnings at the beginning of the panel.
- Defamation, slander, or misinformation about individuals, groups, or organizations are prohibited.
- NSFW/18+ panels are not permitted as this is a family-friendly convention.
- Panels and workshops must comply with all convention policies, including but not limited to our anti-harassment, behaviour, cosplay, and weapons & props policies.
- MiniCon is not responsible for any day-of equipment errors or other issues out of our control.

### Accepted Panelist Submission Approval Process
All accepted panelists are required to submit their final panel presentation and/or a written agenda of their panel contents **at least 2 weeks prior to the event.**  The purpose of this review is to make sure panels are within our panel policies and guidelines.  This review is **not** a critique or revision of your panel contents.

Presentations/agenda **cannot be altered** after they have been approved.  Any major revisions will require a re-approval of contents.
