## Anti-Harassment & Behavior Policy
MiniCon has a zero-tolerance policy for any physical, sexual, verbal, or other forms of harassment, and is committed to providing a safe and welcoming environment for any and all attendees.

### Unacceptable Behavior includes, but is not limited to, the following:
- Intimidating, violent, discriminatory, harassing, derogatory, abusive, or demeaning conduct
- Offensive comments, including comments related to: gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, and disability.
- Stalking
- Non-consensual photography or filming
- Non-consensual physical contact or attention (inclusive of non-consensual hugging)
- Disruption and interference of panels, cosplay meetups, cosplay contest, gaming, or any other programming events
- Damage or vandalism to the premises and any MiniCon equipment or property
- Hate symbols or conduct
- Theft
- Sneaking into off-limit or restricted areas

### Prohibited Behavior
- Alcoholic beverages are prohibited at MiniCon.  Any attendee caught with an alcoholic beverage will be asked to dispose of it immediately.  Any attendee suspected of inebriation will be escorted off the premises immediately and barred from the event without refund.  
- Smoking and/or vaping is not permitted inside the Campus Center building or anywhere on NJIT premises.  For more information on campus smoking policies, please refer to [https://www.njit.edu/policies/smoke-tobacco-free-campus](https://www.njit.edu/policies/smoke-tobacco-free-campus).
- Recreational use of drugs is prohibited at MiniCon. Any attendee caught with recreational drugs will be escorted off the premises immediately and barred from the event without refund.
- Unauthorized solicitation is not permitted. This includes but is not limited to: verbal or physical distribution of any promotional or advertising material, sales or promotions of unauthorized services or goods, distribution of illegal or prohibited goods, campaigning, charitable donations, marketing, or investments.
- Unauthorized parking of vehicles in non-designated areas.
- Playing loud music through portable devices.

### Reporting Harassment
Anyone can report any harassment. To report any harassment or disruptive behavior, please contact the nearest MiniCon security, staff, or volunteer. If an incident escalates beyond MiniCon staff control, law enforcement will be contacted.

### Enforcement
Harassment and unacceptable behaviors are not tolerated by any person at MiniCon, including attendees, guests, panellists, staff, volunteers, vendors, and security. Anyone engaging in any unacceptable behavior will be asked to cease such behavior immediately. If there is any resistance or refusal to stop or correct the behavior, or further continuation of similar behavior, you will be escorted off the premises and barred from the event with no refunds, and will be permanently banned from future MiniCon events. If deemed necessary, or if there are any concerns for the safety of the attendees, MiniCon will contact law enforcement.  All attendees have the right to feel safe at the event.
