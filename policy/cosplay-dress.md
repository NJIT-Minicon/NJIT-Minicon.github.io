## Cosplay Policy and Dress Code
MiniCon is a family-friendly event that provides a safe and fun environment for all attendees.  Due to this, we enforce a dress/cosplay code that meets a standard of appearance that ensures a family-friendly environment and complies to safety and health standards.

## Dress Code
MiniCon requires all attendees to wear shoes, have their bottoms covered and be wearing tops.  Genitilia must be covered, and excessively revealing or transparent clothing that exposes undergarments and/or private areas are not permitted.  Clothing cannot display any hate symbols, explicit profanities, or NSFW/18+ images (ie: ahegao shirts).  If MiniCon staff deem any attire as inappropriate for the event, you will be asked to change attire, or else entry to the event will barred with no refunds.

## Cosplay Policy
Cosplay must meet our dress code as stated above, and must comply with our [weapons & props policy](#weapons).  Nudity, partial nudity, or inadequate coverage of private areas are not permitted.  Body paint, nipple covers, sheer/transparent coverage, or similar substitutes to cover private areas are not accepted, regardless of gender.  Genitilia and private parts must be covered with opaque cloth at all times and must extend two (2) inches above and below all private areas while extending/bending/posing/etc.  Excessively visually violent or shocking cosplay is prohibited.

Cosplay Safety
- Cosplay with any trailing pieces that extend to the floor must be carried when not stationary as it is a tripping hazard.
- Cosplay that have moving/auditory/visual components must comply with our weapons and props policy.  Excessive moving/auditory/visual components are prohibited due to potential safety and health hazards (ie: epilepsy triggers).
- Cosplay should not pose any indoor safety hazards (ie: being so large it obstructs pathways)
