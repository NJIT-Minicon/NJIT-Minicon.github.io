## Judge Applications

Minicon's cosplay judges are individuals who have a passionate love for their skill and craft and are able to interact with the community while also being able to share some knowledge with other cosplayers. You will have the opportunity to become a judge for our Masquerade and Craftsmanship cosplay event. You will also have an opportunity to host your very own panel as well!

Judge guests will be provided with a maximum of 2 additional guest badges, a compensated lunch, and a table of your own to showcase your previous builds or sell any items you might have!
