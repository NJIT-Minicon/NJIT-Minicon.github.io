## Weapons Policy
MiniCon is a family-friendly event and is committed to providing a safe environment for all attendees.  We have a strict weapons and props policy that is strictly enforced.  All weapons, props and devices are subject to inspection and confiscation by MiniCon staff at any time.  For further questions or inquiries on our weapons policy, please email us at [miniconservices@gmail.com](mailto:miniconservices@gmail.com).

### Weapons and Props Check-In
Upon entry to the event, all weapons and props are subject to inspection by MiniCon security and staff.  After picking up your badge, please proceed to the weapons table next to the registration table.  Approved weapons and props will receive a bright-colored sticker, ribbon, or tag for identification purposes.  Any weapons or props that do not pass inspection will be confiscated and held by MiniCon staff until you leave the event.  Noncompliance prevents entry and participation in the event.

### Prohibited Props and Weapons:
- Any metal swords, knives, throwing projectiles, or anything with an edge, regardless of whether it has a sheath or protective covering
- Projectile weapons including but not limited to: firearms, water guns, air rifles, BB guns and pellets, dart guns, bows and crossbows, shuriken, etc
- Imitation firearms, including water guns, are not permitted
- Tasers or stun guns
- Hard paddles
- Weapons or props with chemical or explosive properties such as smoke bombs, flash bombs, glitter bombs, scented bombs, sparklers or fireworks
- Any illegal or real/functional weapons
- Any weapon or item that may maim, damage or cause bodily harm or is deemed a threat or unsafe
- Props or weapons over 6ft in length
- Props or weapons with significantly bright, flashing lights
- Props or weapons that play loud music or have loud sound effects

### Prohibited Weapons Behavior:
- Swinging around props or weapons that may cause injury to individuals
- Leaving props or weapons in areas that would obstruct traffic or cause a tripping hazard
- Mock battles and fights
- Using props or weapons in intimidating or threatening gestures or displays
- Modifying security-approved props or weapons
- Playing instrumental or musical props
- Any significantly bright, flashing lights
- Any other behavior deemed threatening, violent, inappropriate, or a safety hazard

### Permitted Props and Weapons
- Staffs, canes, wands
- Lightweight, plastic, and foam props such as armor, shields, swords, and sabers
- Mock, non-functional bows and crossbows strung loosely
- Decorative props with minimal moving parts, lights or sound and are under 6ft in size
- Mock, non-functional toy guns made of lightweight plastic or foam material with a visible, non-removable orange barrel tip and with the barrel covered with a bright, colorful cap.
  - As per **NJ toy gun laws**, toy guns are required to: 1) be a color other than black, blue, silver, or aluminum; 2) be marked with a non-removable orange stripe that is at least one inch in width and runs the entire length of each side of the gun’s barrel; and 3) have a barrel that is at least one inch in diameter and closed at a distance of at least one-half inch from the barrel’s front end with the same material of which the toy gun or imitation firearm is made. (L. 2019, c.481)
