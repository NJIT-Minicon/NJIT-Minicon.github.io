## Filming and Photography Policy
Any and all recording, filming, or photography of individuals by attendees at MiniCon must be consented to PRIOR to filming.  This is inclusive of attendees, guests, panellists, volunteers, and staff.  Every attendee has the right to decline photography, filming or recording.  Any filming or photography equipment cannot block or obstruct main pathways, emergency exits, entrances, or ramps.  Filming and photography should not disrupt any programmed events or obstruct viewing for attendees.

Permission to film or photograph vendor goods and tables must be obtained by the seller/artist.  Tables with “no photography” signage must be respected.

Use of Meta/Ray-Ban smart glasses or similar wearable recording technology (exclusive of ADA devices) is **not permitted** at MiniCon.  If found wearing these devices, you will be asked to relinquish the items to MiniCon staff, who will hold the devices until either the end of the event or until you leave the event. If you do not consent to this, then you will be asked to leave the event with no refund.  This policy is to protect the safety, comfort, and privacy of our attendees.

All attendees, guests, and panellists consent to being filmed, photographed or recorded by MiniCon staff members, partners, or agents for promotional and archival purposes. By attending MiniCon, you grant permission to MiniCon and its associates to use images and footage without compensation or acknowledgement.  Contracted guests/hires have the right to request access to the filmed content for review prior to public release.
