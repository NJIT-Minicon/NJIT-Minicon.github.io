## Registration and General Rules
### Badge Policy
All attendees, guests, panelists, vendors, staff and volunteers must be wearing their convention badges at all times during the event.  Failure to present a badge upon inspection are grounds for removal from the event.  Replacement badges cost $5 with proof of original purchase.  Without proof of purchase, attendees must pay the full cost for a replacement badge.

### Student/Minor Badges
Students with valid school IDs and minors (17 and younger) are eligible for discounted badges. School IDs must be presented upon badge pickup. For high school and college students, if you are unable to show proof of student status/age and have purchased a ticket online, you will be required to pay the difference for a regular admission badge.

NJIT students with valid NJIT UCIDs get free entry to MiniCon.

### Children & Chaperone Policy
Children under the age of 5 get free entry to MiniCon.
Parents and guardians are required to write emergency contact information on the backs of their children’s badges.  If your child/ward is missing, please contact the nearest MiniCon staff or security.

Minors 14 years and younger must be chaperoned by an adult (21+) at all times during the event.  Unchaperoned minors will not be allowed to participate in the event and will be asked to contact their parent or guardian under the supervision of a MiniCon staff member or volunteer.  If an adult/parent/guardian cannot be found or contacted, law enforcement will be contacted to escort the minor back home.

### Lost Items
Any found items should be handed over to MiniCon staff or volunteers.  All found items will be secured at the registration desk on the first floor in the lobby.

MiniCon is not responsible for any loss, theft or damage to individual property.

### Food Policy
Food and beverages other than water are not permitted in the Artist Alley, Exhibitor Hall, Gaming Room, or Manga Library. Vendors, guests, staff, and volunteers are exempt from this policy.

We ask attendees to consume food and beverages outside of the campus building or at the designated eating areas.
