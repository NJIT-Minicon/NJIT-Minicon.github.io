# MiniCon Convention Policies
Welcome to MiniCon! In order to have a convention that is both safe and fun for all, we have outlined policies listed below that must be followed by all attendees. In the event that you have any questions or need help, please look for our staff wearing staff or volunteer badges on the day of the convention. For inquiries prior to the event, please email us at [miniconservices@gmail.com](mailto:miniconservices@gmail.com). In attending this event, you agree to the policies listed below and understand that the policies may be subject to change without prior notice.
