## Health & Safety Policies
### Mask Policy
MiniCon does not have a mask mandate at this time, but we highly encourage attendees to wear masks. MiniCon reserves the right to ask masked attendees to temporarily remove masks for identification purposes.

### COVID-19
MiniCon acknowledges that the risk of COVID-19 exposure is possible at any public event or gathering.  Please be advised that we cannot question the health status of an individual; therefore, we advise that any attendee who is experiencing symptoms of fever, coughing, shortness of breath, or fatigue within 1-5 days of the event stay at home.  Any individuals who have had known contact with someone who may have COVID-19, or was advised under medical professionals to isolate themselves for COVID-19, should stay at home for the health and safety of other attendees.

By attending MiniCon, you acknowledge and accept that you are attending this event at will with the knowledge that exposure to COVID-19 or any other transmissible diseases is a possibility and that MiniCon takes no responsibility for possible exposure.

### First-Aid, Emergency & Liability Agreements
MiniCon considers the health and safety of all attendees, guests and exhibitors of utmost importance.  First-aid kits are available on site at the registration area, along with emergency granola bars, electrolytes, and water.  Please contact the nearest MiniCon staff or volunteer member if you require minor medical assistance.  **In the case of a medical emergency, please call 911 first and foremost**, then proceed to contact a staff member.

By attending MiniCon, you consent to any on-site emergency medical administration and treatment by a licensed healthcare professional.  Additionally, you waive, relinquish, and release MiniCon from any and all liability, damage, and injury that may arise during and through the participation in this event. You acknowledge that participation in this event is voluntary and at-will, and accept and assume all risks of personal injury, property damage, and theft.

### Evacuation
In the case of an emergency evacuation, the MiniCon security, staff and volunteers will guide attendees to the nearest safety exits to evacuate the building.  Please do not attempt to search for friends or family until you have safely evacuated the building and are at a safe distance.  Please do not attempt to collect your belongings during an emergency evacuation until it is deemed safe to return to the building.
