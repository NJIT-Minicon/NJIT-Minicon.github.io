## Accessibility
MiniCon is committed to universal accessibility for all attendees. The NJIT Campus Center building is fully compliant with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) standards.  For any special accommodation requests or other inquiries, please contact [miniconservices@gmail.com](mailto:miniconservices@gmail.com).

### Complimentary Caregiver Policy
Attendees with a caregiver/assistant may receive one (1) complimentary badge with the purchase of a regular admission badge.  Please indicate on the registration form that a caregiver badge is requested.  Badges may be picked up on the day of the event at the registration table.

### Accessible Seating Policy
MiniCon offers accessible seating as available. Accessible seating is on a first-come, first-served basis and cannot be guaranteed.

### ASL and Mobility
Unfortunately, MiniCon does not offer on-site ASL, assisted listening services, or mobility equipment at this time.

### Service Animal Policy
Registered service animals recognized by the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) are welcome if leashed.  Emotional Support Animals (ESA) and other pets are not covered under the ADA and do not qualify as service animals, and are not allowed at MiniCon.

### Sensory Quiet Room
MiniCon offers a quiet room area on the second floor of the Campus Center building open to all attendees. The room offers a quiet space with white noise, coloring activities and fidget toys.

### Accessible Parking
MiniCon offers accessible parking at NJIT’s parking decks, however, these parking spaces cannot be held for reservations. Parking spots marked as handicapped spaces are available on a first-come, first-served basis.
